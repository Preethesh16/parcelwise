Reliability measures outcomes

Reliability = 60% on-time delivery rate + 40% successful delivery rate. After delivery, customer feedback records both outcomes and a 1–5 care rating. Quality converts that care rating to a 0–100 scale.

Feedback improves the relevant route

Each company starts with 40 simulated historical deliveries’ worth of baseline evidence. Feedback from the same origin → destination has full weight; other routes contribute 20% weight. One review therefore changes a baseline by at most about 2.5 percentage points. Reverse routes are separate.

Consistent normalization

Price score = 100 ÷ (1 + price ÷ 250). Speed score = max(0, 100 × (1 − (days − 1) ÷ 6)). Reliability and quality use their estimated percentages. These fixed scales stay consistent even when a carrier is ineligible.

Eligibility and pricing

Billable weight is the larger of actual kg or length × width × height ÷ 5,000. A supports up to 25 kg, B up to 15 kg, and C up to 30 kg. B does not serve Jaipur; C does not serve Kolkata. A offers 15% off for billable weights up to 5 kg; C offers 10% off routes over 800 km. Distance is a rough coordinate-based estimate; transit estimates use calendar days.

A, B and C are fictional delivery companies, not individual couriers. No live bookings, payments, carrier integrations or customer notifications occur. This local demo has no authentication.
